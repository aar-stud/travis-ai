# TRAVIS Bank: Master Policy Document

This document outlines the standard operating procedures, customer requirements, and product policies for TRAVIS Bank in accordance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines.

## 1. Account Opening

### Savings Accounts
To open a savings account, customers must provide a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN card, Passport, or Voter ID), address proof (utility bill or Aadhaar), one recent passport-size photograph, and a minimum opening deposit. NRI customers additionally require a valid visa and overseas address proof. Customers must be at least 18 years old to open a regular savings account in their own name; minors can hold accounts jointly with a guardian.

### Minimum Balance Requirements
- **Regular Savings Accounts:** Require a minimum balance of Rs 1,000.
- **Premium Savings Accounts:** Require a minimum balance of Rs 5,000.
- **Zero-Balance Accounts:** Available for students, senior citizens, and Jan Dhan Yojana beneficiaries with no minimum balance requirement.

### Account Activation
Account opening typically takes 24 to 48 hours after the submission of all required documents. Video KYC accounts can be opened instantly online. Branch-based accounts are activated within one working day. Customers will receive their account number and debit card by post or email.

### Joint Accounts
Joint accounts can be opened with a maximum of three account holders. All holders must submit their individual KYC documents and sign the account opening form. The account can operate under modes such as "Either or Survivor", "Former or Survivor", or "Jointly".

## 2. KYC (Know Your Customer) Compliance

KYC is a mandatory process required by the Reserve Bank of India to verify the identity and address of bank customers, helping prevent fraud, money laundering, and terrorist financing. KYC must be completed at account opening and periodically updated.

### Accepted Documents
- **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar card, PAN card, Passport, Voter ID, and Driving License.
- **Address Proof:** Aadhaar, Passport, utility bills (not older than 3 months), bank account statements, and registered rent agreements.

### KYC Updates
Customers can update their KYC by visiting their home branch with original documents, using the video KYC option on the mobile app, or through net banking for minor updates. The required documents are a valid photo ID and address proof. Updates are free of charge and process within 2 to 3 working days.

### Non-Compliance
If KYC is not completed within the specified period, the account may be frozen for debit transactions (credits will still be allowed). Once KYC is completed, restrictions are lifted within 48 hours. Customers receive SMS and email reminders before any restrictions are applied.

## 3. Loan Products & Policies

TRAVIS Bank offers Home Loans, Personal Loans, Auto Loans, Education Loans, Business Loans, Gold Loans, Agriculture/Crop Loans, and Medical Loans. General eligibility, documentation, and repayment terms for each product are described below.

### 3.1 Home Loans
**Eligibility:** Minimum age 18 years, maximum age 70 years at loan maturity. Available to salaried and self-employed applicants; a co-applicant may be added to improve eligibility.
**Required Documents:** Identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN), address proof, passport-size photographs, and property documents (sale agreement, title deed, encumbrance certificate). Salaried applicants require salary slips for the last 3 months, Form 16, and bank statements for the last 6 months. Self-employed applicants require ITR for the last 2 years and business proof.

### 3.2 Personal Loans
**Eligibility:** Minimum age 21 years, maximum age 60 years at loan maturity. Minimum monthly income of Rs 15,000 for salaried employees, minimum 1 year with current employer, and a CIBIL score of 650 or above.
**Required Documents:** Identity proof, address proof, salary slips or income proof, bank statements for the last 3 months, and passport-size photographs.

### 3.3 Auto Loans
**Eligibility:** Minimum age 21 years, maximum age 65 years at loan maturity. Minimum monthly income of Rs 15,000, and a CIBIL score of 650 or above.
**Required Documents:** Identity proof, address proof, income proof (salary slips or ITR), bank statements, quotation/proforma invoice of the vehicle, and passport-size photographs.

### 3.4 Education Loans
**Eligibility:** Applicant must be an Indian national securing admission to a recognized institution (in India or abroad) for a graduate, postgraduate, or professional/technical course. A parent or guardian is typically required as a co-applicant.
**Required Documents:** Identity and address proof of both student and co-applicant, admission letter from the institution, fee structure/cost of the course, academic records (mark sheets/certificates), income proof of the co-applicant, and collateral or third-party guarantee for loans above a specified threshold.
**Approval Timeline:** 15 working days after document submission.

### 3.5 Business Loans
**Eligibility:** Business must be operational for a minimum period (typically 2 years) with satisfactory turnover and repayment capacity.
**Required Documents:** Identity and address proof of the proprietor/partners/directors, business registration and licenses, ITR and financial statements for the last 2 years, bank statements for the last 6 to 12 months, and GST returns where applicable.

### 3.6 Gold Loans
**Eligibility:** Open to individuals aged 18 years and above who own gold ornaments or coins of acceptable purity.
**Required Documents:** Identity proof, address proof, and the gold items to be pledged for valuation. No income proof is typically required.

### 3.7 Agriculture/Crop Loans
**Eligibility:** Available to farmers, tenant farmers, and agricultural laborers who own or cultivate agricultural land. Includes short-term crop loans (e.g., Kisan Credit Card) and term loans for farm equipment or land development.
**Required Documents:** Identity proof, address proof, land ownership or tenancy documents (land records/7-12 extract or equivalent), details of crops cultivated and cropping pattern, and passport-size photographs. Loans against Kisan Credit Card require the KCC application form in addition to the above.

### 3.8 Medical Loans
**Eligibility:** Available to individuals requiring funds for planned or emergency medical treatment for self or a dependent family member. Minimum age 21 years; income proof required except in emergency cases, which may be processed with minimal documentation for faster disbursal.
**Required Documents:** Identity proof, address proof, treatment cost estimate from the hospital, relevant medical reports/doctor's prescription, and income proof (salary slips or bank statements). Loans may be disbursed directly to the hospital.

### Interest Rates & Approval (Personal Loans)
Personal loan interest rates range from 10.5% to 18% per annum depending on the credit score and income (CIBIL > 750 qualifies for the best rates). Processing fees are 1% to 2% of the loan amount. Loan tenures range from 12 to 60 months.

Approval timelines:
- **Personal Loans:** 24 to 72 hours for pre-approved customers; 5 to 7 working days for new applicants.
- **Home Loans:** 7 to 15 working days after document submission.
- **Education Loans:** 15 working days after document submission.

Customers receive SMS and email updates at each stage of the approval process.

### EMI (Equated Monthly Installment)
An EMI is a fixed monthly payment that includes both principal repayment and interest. It is calculated based on the principal amount, the monthly interest rate, and the total number of installments. Customers can use the EMI calculator on the bank website or mobile app to get an exact figure.

### Repayment & Prepayment
Prepayment and foreclosure are allowed on all loan products. Floating rate loans carry no prepayment charges per RBI guidelines. Fixed-rate loans may incur a prepayment charge of 2% to 4% of the outstanding principal. Partial prepayment is permitted after 6 EMI payments. Full foreclosure is allowed at any time.

## 4. Cards

### 4.1 Credit Cards

**Eligibility:** Minimum age of 18 years, minimum annual income of Rs 2.5 lakhs, and a CIBIL score of 650 or above.

**How to Apply:** Customers can apply online through the bank website or mobile app, at any branch, or through pre-approved offers extended to existing customers. Existing bank customers receive faster processing due to pre-verified KYC and income data.

**Dispatch & Delivery:** Once approved, cards are dispatched within 3 to 5 working days by registered post or courier, with SMS/email tracking details provided. Estimated delivery is 7 to 10 working days from dispatch. If the card is not received within 15 days, customers should contact customer care to request a replacement.

**Billing Cycle & Payments:** The billing cycle is 30 days. A statement is generated on the statement date each month showing all transactions and the total amount due. The payment due date is 15 to 20 days after the statement date. Full payment by the due date incurs no interest charges.

**Activation & Security:** New cards are activated by calling the customer care number on the back of the card, or via the mobile app, net banking portal, or ATM, and setting a 4-digit PIN. The card is ready to use immediately after activation, though first-time transactions may require OTP verification sent to the registered mobile number. If a card is lost or stolen, it must be immediately blocked via the 24x7 customer care helpline, mobile banking app, or net banking; the loss should also be reported to the nearest police station. A replacement card is issued within 5 to 7 working days.

### 4.2 Debit Cards

**Eligibility:** Issued by default to all savings and current account holders aged 18 years and above at the time of account opening. Minors with guardian-operated accounts may be issued a debit card with usage restrictions, subject to branch discretion.

**How to Apply:** A debit card is generated automatically as part of the account opening process. Existing customers without a debit card, or those requiring a replacement (lost, damaged, or upgrade), can apply via the mobile app, net banking, or branch visit.

**Activation:** New debit cards are activated at the first ATM transaction using the default PIN sent by post, or by generating a PIN through the mobile app, net banking, or ATM "Generate PIN" option using OTP verification.

**Usage & Limits:** Debit cards can be used for ATM withdrawals, point-of-sale purchases, and online transactions. Daily withdrawal and spending limits are set by default and can be modified by the customer through the mobile app or net banking, subject to bank ceilings.

**Loss or Theft:** Lost or stolen debit cards should be blocked immediately via the 24x7 customer care helpline, mobile app, or net banking. A replacement card is issued within 5 to 7 working days.

## 5. Fund Transfers

- **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service):** Instant, real-time transfers available 24x7, including weekends and public holidays. Maximum limit of Rs 5 lakhs per transaction. Nominal charges apply per transaction. Ideal for urgent transfers at any time of day.
- **NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer):** Nationwide one-to-one transfer system processed in half-hourly batches, available 24x7 including weekends and holidays. No minimum or maximum limit. Online NEFT is free of charge for individual customers; branch-initiated transfers may carry a nominal fee.
- **RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement):** Real-time gross settlement for high-value transactions, preferred for large urgent inter-bank transfers. Minimum transfer of Rs 2 lakhs, no maximum limit. Available 24x7, including weekends and public holidays. Online RTGS is free of charge; branch-initiated transfers may carry a nominal fee.

### 5.1 UPI (Unified Payments Interface)

**What UPI Is:** UPI is a real-time payment system that allows instant money transfers using a virtual payment address (VPA), QR code, or mobile number, without the need to enter bank account or IBAN details. UPI is available 24x7, including weekends and holidays, and most transactions are free of charge. Customers must be 18 years or older with an active savings or current account to use UPI.

**How to Set Up a UPI ID:**
1. Download the official bank mobile app (or a compatible UPI app) from the App Store or Google Play Store.
2. Link the bank account using the registered mobile number, which is verified via an automatic SMS check.
3. Create a UPI ID (VPA), typically formatted as the customer's mobile number or chosen handle followed by the bank's UPI handle (e.g., `9876543210@travisbank`).
4. Set a 4- to 6-digit UPI PIN by entering the last 6 digits of the debit card number, its expiry date, and an OTP sent to the registered mobile number.

**How to Use UPI:**
- To send money, enter the recipient's UPI ID, mobile number, or scan their QR code, enter the amount, and authenticate with the UPI PIN.
- To receive money, share your UPI ID or QR code with the sender; no PIN is required to receive funds.
- The standard limit for most UPI payments is Rs 1 lakh per day; select verified-merchant categories (such as capital markets, insurance, education, and healthcare) may allow higher per-transaction and daily caps under NPCI guidelines. Exact limits may vary by account type and are visible within the app.
- A transaction history and digital receipt are available within the app immediately after each transfer.

## 6. Account Services & Digital Banking

### Account Updates
- **Mobile Number:** Must be updated at the home branch with an Aadhaar card and a self-attested copy (branch visit required for security reasons). Takes 24 to 48 hours, with confirmation sent to both old and new numbers.
- **Email Address:** Can be updated via mobile app or net banking. Takes 24 to 48 hours, with confirmation sent to both old and new email addresses.

### Cheque Books
Cheque books can be requested via the mobile banking app, net banking, ATM, or branch visit. Standard 10-leaf cheque books are free; additional cheque books may carry a nominal charge. Delivery takes 5 to 7 working days to the registered address.

### Unauthorized Transactions
Unauthorized transactions should be reported immediately via customer care or the dispute resolution option in the mobile app/net banking. Reporting within 3 days qualifies for zero liability. The bank investigates within 10 working days, and provisional credit may be issued during the investigation.

### Account Closure
To close an account, the customer must visit the home branch with the passbook, debit card, unused cheque leaves, and a signed account closure form, and ensure the balance is zero (or withdraw any remaining balance). The closure process takes 7 working days. Pre-closure charges may apply if the account is closed within 12 months of opening.

### 6.1 Net Banking (Internet Banking)

**What It Is:** Net banking is the bank's web-based portal that allows customers to manage accounts, transfer funds, pay bills, and update select details online, available 24x7. Customers must be 18 years or older and hold an active account to register.

**How to Start Net Banking:**
1. Visit the bank website and select the "Register for Net Banking" option, or request registration during account opening at the branch.
2. Enter the account number, registered mobile number, and debit card details for verification.
3. Confirm identity via an OTP sent to the registered mobile number.
4. Set a login ID/customer ID and a transaction password of at least 8 characters, including letters, numbers, and a special character.
5. Registration can also be completed by visiting the branch and submitting a physical registration form, without needing a debit card.

**Forgotten Password Recovery:** Passwords can be reset using the "Forgot Password" option on the login page, using the customer ID or account number, registered mobile number (for OTP), and debit card details. Customers may also visit the branch or call customer care.

### Digital Security
Mobile banking is secured with 256-bit SSL encryption, two-factor authentication (OTP), biometric login, session timeout, and device binding. Customers should never share their OTP, MPIN, or password with anyone, and should only download the official app from the App Store or Google Play Store.

## 7. Fixed Deposits (FD)

Fixed deposits offer guaranteed returns and capital protection with tenures from 7 days to 10 years. Available to individuals aged 18 years and above; minors may hold FDs jointly with a guardian.
- Loans against FD are available up to 90% of the deposit amount.
- Premature withdrawals are allowed, subject to a small penalty.
- **Interest Rates by Tenure:**
  - 7–45 days: 3.5%–4.5%
  - 46–180 days: 4.5%–5.5%
  - 181–365 days: 5.5%–6.5%
  - 1–3 years: 6.5%–7%
  - Senior citizens receive an additional 0.5% on all tenures.

## 8. Customer Service & Grievance Redressal

### Timings
- **Branch Timings:** Monday to Friday (10 AM to 4 PM), Saturday (10 AM to 2 PM). Closed on Sundays and public holidays.
- **Customer Care:** Available 24x7 for emergencies (e.g., card blocking). General queries are handled from 8 AM to 10 PM daily, including weekends.

### Complaints
Complaints can be raised via the mobile app, net banking, customer care, email, or branch manager. Each complaint generates a ticket number for tracking. Resolution takes 5 to 7 working days.

### Escalation
If unsatisfied with the resolution, customers can escalate to the Banking Ombudsman of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). The Ombudsman handles disputes between customers and banks, and the service is free of charge.
